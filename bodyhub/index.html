Inside Body Hub

Everyday habits. A longer view.

01 / Daily records

Start with your day.

Keep movement, meals, hydration and recovery in one place. Review the records you have logged on your Today screen.

02 / Training

Remember the details.

Record workouts and individual exercise sets, then return to your history to review your training.

03 / Progress

See the pattern.

Track weight and body measurements over time. Charts and summaries help you review the information you record.

04 / Connections

Choose what connects.

Optional Health Connect imports bring supported records into Body Hub. Connections depend on device support and the permissions you grant.

05 / Local records

Your information, at hand.

Your profile and saved records are stored on your device. Body Hub does not provide developer-operated cloud synchronization or a complete backup-and-restore service.

06 / Data controls

Understand your choices.

Find permission, export and deletion guidance in the app’s resources. Learn what is included in exports and how to manage local and connected records.
