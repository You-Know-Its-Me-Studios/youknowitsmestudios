Useful context.
Clear choices.

Optional Health Connect imports bring supported records into Body Hub. On-device AI can help with summaries and suggestions using relevant saved information.

Your local profile is not an online account. Body Hub does not provide developer-operated cloud synchronization or a complete backup-and-restore service.
