Inside Strata

A home screen with a little more intention.

01 / One surface

Keep everything connected.

Move through your launcher in one continuous scroll. Favorites, groups and apps stay in the same flow.

02 / Glance Deck

Useful context, close by.

Choose modules for weather, calendar, media and system information. Availability depends on device support and the permissions you grant.

03 / Favorites + groups

Organize in place.

Keep essential apps in Favorites and create custom groups inline with the rest of your launcher.

04 / Navigation

Find your way, faster.

Jump through your app index with the right-edge navigation rail, or search when you know what you need.

05 / Personalization

Set the atmosphere.

Choose a theme, tune custom theme settings and adjust app density to suit your home screen.

06 / Your choices

Make space for your day.

Arrange the launcher around the information and apps you want to reach. Review permissions and data handling in the privacy policy.
