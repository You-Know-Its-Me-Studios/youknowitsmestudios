Why it’s different

Not another stack
of home screens.

Conventional launchers divide your setup into pages. Strata keeps it connected: one surface where glanceable information, Favorites, groups, and apps share the same vertical flow.

02 / Favorites + groups

Organize in place.

Keep essential apps in Favorites, then create custom groups that live inline with the rest of your launcher. Organization stays visible and connected—without sending you through nested folders or separate pages.

03 / Navigation rail
