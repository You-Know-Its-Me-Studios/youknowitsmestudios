About the studio

Small ideas.
Useful tools.

You Know Its Me Studios is an independent software studio building thoughtful tools for a more intentional, productive and private digital life.

Our Android projects bring useful information closer: organize your home screen with Strata, record and reflect on fitness and wellness with Body Hub, and understand your flight with Nadir.

What we build

Tools that fit your day.

Strata / Android launcher

A more intentional home screen.

Strata brings apps, Favorites, custom groups and a Glance Deck into one continuous surface. Themes, search and a navigation rail help you arrange and find what matters to you.

Explore Strata

Body Hub / Fitness & wellness

Record, reflect and see your progress.

Body Hub brings meals, workouts and everyday wellness records together on your device. Review your history, follow changes over time and choose which supported records to connect through Health Connect.

Explore Body Hub

Nadir / Passenger flight companion

Follow your own flight.

Nadir uses your phone's GPS and sensors to show live flight information and record the actual trip. Offline maps and local geographic context keep it useful without aircraft Wi-Fi, with optional online weather and aviation detail.

Explore Nadir
