01 / Before you write

Basic troubleshooting

  1. 01
    Restart Strata

    Open another app, return to the home screen, and check whether the issue remains.

  2. 02
    Restart your device

    A restart can clear temporary Android system or launcher state.

  3. 03
    Review permissions

    In Android settings, confirm that permissions required by the feature are still enabled.

  4. 04
    Check your defaults

    If Strata does not open as expected, confirm that it is selected as your default Home app.

02 / Bug reports

What to include

The clearest reports include enough context to reproduce the issue. When possible, send:

  • Strata app version
  • Android version
  • Device manufacturer and model
  • A short description of what you expected and what happened
  • The steps that led to the issue
